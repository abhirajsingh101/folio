SECTION 01What you asked for

Twenty-seven of your forty-three services are off the monolith. The sixteen that remain are not the hard part — the shared orders schema underneath them is, and it is now the thing setting your release cadence.

Your Q3 report names the extraction as the Q4 keystone and says the team cannot staff it alongside the remaining service moves. That is the work this proposal covers, and only that: we take the schema, you keep the services.

The constraint you gave us is the one that shapes everything below. A freeze is not available — peak season starts on 2026.11.24 and the order path has to keep shipping through it. So the extraction runs behind a dual-write, and the cutover is a switch you can throw back for the first two weeks after it, rather than a migration you commit to on the night.

What changes when it is done: a schema change to orders stops being a cross-team release window, and the sixteen remaining services can move independently. The decision in front of you is whether to start before peak or after it — we recommend before, for the reason in the chart below.

SECTION 02Scope

What is out matters as much as what is in, so both are here rather than the second one being discovered in week six.

Included

  • Extraction of the 41 orders tables into their own instance, with the 9 cross-service writers routed through an API.
  • Dual-write with a reconciliation report you can read daily.
  • Two cutover rehearsals against a production-sized copy.
  • Two weeks of reversible operation after the switch.
  • The cutover runbook, and a rehearsal your on-call team runs.

Not included

  • The sixteen remaining service moves — yours, and unblocked by this.
  • Application changes above the data layer.
  • Hosting and licence costs, which stay in your name.
  • Operation after handover. We are not on your rota.
