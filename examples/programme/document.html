A note on the second half

The reprise is played from the west gallery with the lights out. Nothing is wrong; please stay in your seat until the bell stops sounding, which takes about ninety seconds after the last stroke.

Ama Bell wrote the Hymn after the tenor bell of this chapel cracked in 2024. The recording of that bell, made three days before it failed, is the only part of the evening that is not live.

The series

The Nightjar Sessions run eight times a year in this chapel, always at 19:30, always without an interval, and always with one piece nobody in the room has heard before. There is no season ticket and no allocated seating; the chapel holds ninety-four.

Practicalities

The chapel is cold; keep your coat. Step-free entry is through the vestry door on Mill Lane. Please do not applaud between the Ligeti and the silence.

The collection

Tonight's retiring collection goes to the bell fund — cash, or the card reader by the door. Last month's total was £412 and the running total is posted in the porch.
