Database failover

Promote the standby and return service, with the checks that say whether it worked.

Stop

Step 4 is irreversible: once the standby is promoted the old primary cannot be re-attached without a full resync. Confirm replication lag is under 5 seconds before starting, and say what makes this the wrong procedure to run.

SECTION 01Preconditions

Every line here is a check, not a note. If one fails, stop and escalate rather than continuing — name who.

  • Incident channel open and the on-call lead is aware.
  • Replication lag under 5 seconds (SELECT now() - last_replay).
  • Most recent base backup verified within the last 24 hours.
  • Maintenance page ready to publish.

Access required

Production database role failover, and the deploy pipeline. Ask in the incident channel rather than escalating your own access.

SECTION 02Procedure

  1. Announce the start. Post in the incident channel with this document's number and revision. Expect: acknowledgement from the on-call lead.
  2. Publish the maintenance page. deploy maintenance --on. Expect: the status page shows Maintenance within 30 seconds.
  3. Stop writes to the primary. svc pause writer. Expect: the writer queue drains to zero; confirm before continuing.
  4. Promote the standby. pgctl promote standby-2. Expect: the node reports primary and accepts a test write. This is the irreversible step.
  5. Repoint the application. deploy config set db.host=standby-2, then roll the web tier. Expect: error rate returns to baseline within two minutes.
  6. Lift maintenance. deploy maintenance --off, and announce in the channel.

SECTION 03Verification and rollback

Record the measured value, not a tick. A checklist of ticks is not evidence that the service recovered.

If a check fails

Rollback

Before step 4, rollback is svc resume writer and lifting maintenance. After step 4 there is no rollback: the old primary must be rebuilt as a standby (SOP-021), which takes about four hours. Escalate to the database owner instead of improvising.

After the incident

  • File the timeline in the incident record within 24 hours.
  • Raise a revision to this document if any step read wrong at 03:00.
